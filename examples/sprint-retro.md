# Sprint 43 retrospective

## What went well

- The MCP server shipped a day early

- Demo videos got great feedback from the team
- Fewer meetings, more focus time

## What did not go well

## Ideas

- A rotating "pipeline doctor" each sprint
- Quarantine flaky tests automatically
